Title: Privacy policy

AcoustID is an open source project run from the European Union. This page
explains what personal data acoustid.org and the AcoustID API collect, why, and
what you can do about it.

In short:

- You can browse acoustid.org without an account.
- We collect only what we need to run the service and keep it from being
  abused.
- We never sell your data, and we never show your email address publicly.
- Fingerprints and metadata you submit become part of the public AcoustID
  database. They are published without any link to your account.

## Who we are

AcoustID is run by Lukáš Lalinský through AcoustID OÜ, a company registered in
Estonia, which is responsible for your data. For anything about privacy, write
to [info@acoustid.org](mailto:info@acoustid.org).

## What we collect

**When you use the website or the API**, our servers receive your IP address,
the user agent of your browser or application, and the request itself. We use
this to run the service, to count usage per application, and to find and stop
abuse.

**When you create an account**, we store:

- the identity you signed in with: your MusicBrainz account, your Google
  account, or your email address
- your email address, if you sign in with it or add it to your account
- your API key
- the IP address you created the account from

We use your email address only to sign you in and to contact you about your
account or your use of AcoustID. We do not send newsletters.

**When an application uses your API key**, we count your lookups per
application and per day. We use this to see unusual usage.

**When you submit fingerprints**, we store them with the metadata you send and
a link to your account. The fingerprints and metadata are published as part of
the AcoustID database. The link to your account is not published.

**When you register an application**, we store its name, its website and the
contact email you give us.

## Who else handles your data

We use a few services to run AcoustID. They process data only on our behalf.

- **Hetzner** hosts our servers in the EU. Our databases are stored there.
- **Cloudflare** sits in front of acoustid.org and the API and sees all
  traffic to them.
- **Mailgun** sends our sign-in and confirmation emails.
- **Grafana Cloud** stores our server logs and metrics.
- **Backblaze** stores encrypted backups.
- **MusicBrainz** and **Google** handle sign-in, if you choose them. We receive
  only your account identifier from them, and from Google your email address.

Some of these companies are based in the United States. Transfers to them are
covered by standard data protection agreements.

We do not share personal data with anyone else, unless the law requires it.

## How long we keep it

- Server logs: 14 days.
- Lookup counts per user: 90 days.
- User agents and IP addresses per application: 90 days.
- Your account, including the IP address it was created from: until you ask us
  to delete it.
- Submissions: permanently, because they are part of the database. We never
  delete them from the public dataset, even on request. If you delete your
  account, the link between your submissions and you is removed.
- Backups: up to 6 months.

## Cookies

acoustid.org uses one cookie, to keep you signed in. We use no analytics or
tracking cookies.

## Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of your data, to correct it, or to delete your
account. Write to [info@acoustid.org](mailto:info@acoustid.org). You also have
the right to complain to the data protection authority in your country.

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the date below.
